# SPINE

Canon. Acts gate which ranks are open. Nothing here contradicts a ladder.

## The clock

Day 1 to day 84, three months. The season is the villain: she has 84 days of hours and
four people who want them. Acts are hard gates on rank.

| Act | Days | Ranks open | What the act is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 The hours | 1-14 | 1-3 | Establish all four. No physical contact at all. The player learns the calendar is the enemy. |
| 2 The gap | 15-42 | 4-6 | First deliberate touch, first kiss, first act. The porn discovery lands here. |
| 3 Collision | 43-70 | 7-9 | The brothers start noticing. Dinner table becomes the best and worst room in the house. |
| 4 Reckoning | 71-84 | 10 | Eric. The choice. Endings. |

Rank 6 is the pivot: it is the first act, and it is also where Tyler and Miles begin to
overlap. Nothing explicit before day 15.

## Suspicion

`GameState.suspicion`, 0-100. Rises when she is caught-adjacent: a witness, a schedule
collision, an early return. Falls slowly on days she spends with nobody.

- Under 30: nobody is looking.
- 30-60: someone is looking. Dinner table scenes get colder.
- Over 60 entering act 4: the reveal is forced. No choice.

Eric's r9 raises it by being home more. Rex's r9 raises it via Miles as witness. The
brothers' r10 collision raises it hard.

## The four routes are not equal

- Miles and Tyler are the spine. Their r10s are the same event from two sides: each one
  says it out loud and the other is standing in the doorway. Both cannot be reached
  without the other finding out.
- Eric is the pressure gauge, not a rival. His ladder is attention, and his rank is the
  same number as her guilt.
- Rex is parallel and never blocks anyone. He is the only route you can finish while
  everything else is falling apart, and the only one nobody counts as cheating.

## Act 3 set piece

A dinner table scene that fires when any two of {Miles, Tyler, Eric} are at rank 7 or
above. All four at the table. Nobody can do anything. The player picks which version of
herself to be and it costs her with the other two.

## Act 4 endings

| Ending | Condition |
|---|---|
| Complicit | Eric at rank 8+, suspicion under 60. He works it out and asks. "What does that say about us." |
| Kept | Suspicion under 30. Nobody ever knows. Four separate lives, no resolution. |
| Caught | Suspicion 60+. Forced, unplanned, mid-scene. |
| One | A single bond at rank 9+ and the rest at 5 or below. She chose the hours. |
